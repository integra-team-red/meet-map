MeetMap

MeetMap is a mobile-first web application that helps users discover events and find the best way to spend their free time. Users can explore upcoming events through a personalized event feed, search and filter events, or discover them directly on an interactive map.

Each event provides essential information such as its date, location, tags, age requirements, capacity, rating, and description. Users can join events, view other participants’ profiles, and keep track of the events they have attended.

MeetMap also allows users to create their own events, manage their profiles and interests, and interact with a community of people with similar interests. Administrators help keep the platform safe and reliable by reviewing reported events and managing inappropriate or cancelled content.

Explore. Connect. Experience more with MeetMap.
